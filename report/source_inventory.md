# Literature inventory and classification

The five supplied primary PDFs were inventoried **before external searching**; see [paper_inventory.md](../paper_inventory.md). They remain authoritative for what those authors wrote, including errors. External papers do not replace their contents. Retrieval records beside downloaded files preserve URL, timestamp, byte length and SHA-256. The search is documented and reproducible at the query-family level, but is not an exhaustive subscription-database systematic review.

Categories: **A** foundational method; **B** modification; **C** comparative; **D** review/background synthesis; **E** experimental benchmark; **F** contains reported exact/reference optimal solutions; **G** modification inspiration. Category F means the source reports reference optima, not that every printed number survived our LP audit. Access codes: full = full paper read; partial = abstract/preview or selected source sections only; data = author-supplied supplement, not an independent peer-reviewed article.

No extra method was promoted to a main candidate solely because its abstract claims superiority. A dedicated systematic review was not fully audited; the comparison papers provide useful background synthesis but should not be mislabelled as exhaustive systematic reviews.

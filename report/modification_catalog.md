# Modification opportunities and novelty audit

Everything proposed here is **C: our analysis/design**, unless labelled an existing published rule. A promising mechanism is not automatically a new contribution. A = existing published rule; B = similar published ideas; C = combination of existing ideas; D = no close match found in the searched literature (not a novelty proof). No direction below is claimed novel.

Notation: A_ij=min(s_i,d_j) uses remaining margins; P is the original line penalty; e_i=sum_j X_ij-s_i; q_D/q_R are the donor/receiver-satisfaction quantities in the algorithm contract; T<=m+n-1 is the number of positive constructive allocations; B is one baseline construction. "Easy" means short Python implementation and a small matrix can be calculated by hand; it is not evidence of better performance. Search date and access limitations are in [search_log.md](search_log.md).

The tables provide temporary names, the precise original/changed decision, formula, rationale/expected benefit, possible weakness, added work, parameter requirement, implementation/manual ease, prior-art status, and experiment status. "Untested" means no effectiveness claim.

## What the novelty search rules out

- **Top-2/top-3 alone:** already central to supplied THP (2021) and [IVAM (2011)](https://doi.org/10.3390/mca16020370). Their precise penalties, transformed matrix and secondary choices differ; they are not synonyms for the tested completion rule.
- **Allocation-aware rules alone:** THP already uses cost times allocation. [Rahman et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.14569/IJACSA.2024.0150928) explicitly combines feasible capacity with distribution/opportunity indicators. KSAM and weighted opportunity-cost papers also make blanket novelty claims unsafe.
- **Maximum allocation to break a least-cost tie:** explicitly appears in [iLCM (2016)](https://www.scienpress.com/download.asp?ID=1899). Its full algorithm additionally continues along a residual row/column and has further tie rules; the experiment is a single-component variant, not a reproduction of complete iLCM.
- **Changing TDSM's exponent to 1.5:** belongs to the original published parameter family.
- **Row/column range variants:** [MRCM (2021)](https://jmcms.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/24093159/6.-3.-JMCMS-Jan.-2021-THE-MAXIMUM-RANGE-COLUMN-METHOD-H.-B.-Kalhoro-et-al-1.pdf) and [Arifin's thesis](https://eprints.undip.ac.id/84177/) predate Wireko's version. The tested best-of-orientations rule is conservatively a combination of existing ideas.
- **One-step heuristic completion:** [Bertsekas, Tsitsiklis & Wu (1997)](https://www.mit.edu/~jnt/Papers/J066-97-rollout.pdf) already develops rollout. This project uses deterministic arithmetic and repeated simple heuristics; it does not train a model or require reinforcement learning. No closely matching implementation of the exact VAM/THP two-cell policy was found in the searched TP sources, but this is **C**, not a claim that rollout or top-k selection is new.

The inaccessible full OPSEARCH comparison and original JHM paper are material novelty limitations. A thesis contribution should be framed as a carefully specified adaptation with reproducible comparative evidence unless a fuller institutional literature search establishes a narrower original claim.

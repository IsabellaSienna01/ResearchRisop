## Algorithm and reporting issues already visible in supplied sources

- L01: BCE uses a modulo of a supply difference by a cost difference, with equal-cost branches. Units and scaling sensitivity deserve analysis; do not replace its rule with an unrelated heuristic.
- L02: Step 8 says equal cost–supply products satisfy SLC, but the worked narrative says FLC; Table 5 actually satisfies SLC. The allocation list says X21=10 while Table 6 places 10 at X23. The N04 prose says 3×5/1580 while Table 8 says 3×3/555. Preserve these discrepancies. Step 6's “cost increase” is destination cost × quantity, not net cost change.
- L03: original scope is balanced TP but Step 1 explicitly says to balance unequal totals. Choosing among tied column minima and final residual ties is not completely specified. Treat deterministic completion rules as implementation conventions.
- L04: Example 1 reduced column entries disagree with subtracting column minima from the original matrix; Example 2 includes 13 where 12−1=11. “Max zero cells” is ambiguous. Worked totals and Table 9 conflict. Independent LP verification is essential.
- L05: the abstract suggests unambiguous selection; the body explicitly admits residual arbitrary choices. Top-2 penalty levels and cost × allocation are therefore **already published**, not a new project idea.
